Simplified (English): Every Union territory is administered by the President acting, to such extent as he thinks fit, through an administrator appointed by him with a specified designation, unless Parliament provides otherwise by law. Simplified (English): Inserted by the Constitution (Sixty-ninth Amendment) Act, 1991, this article designates the Union territory of Delhi as the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi, with its administrator designated as the Lieutenant Governor. It establishes a Legislative Assembly chosen by direct election from territorial constituencies. Crucially, the Constitution (One-hundred and Sixth Amendment) Act, 2023 introduced provisions to reserve seats for women, ensuring that nearly one-third of the total direct seats (including those reserved for Scheduled Castes) are reserved for women. The Assembly has power to make laws on State and Concurrent List matters in so far as they apply to Union territories, except for Public Order, Police, and Land (Entries 1, 2, and 18 of the State List). In case of conflict, Parliament's laws are repugnant and prevail over Assembly laws, unless the Assembly's law was reserved for the President and received Presidential assent. Under Clause (4), a Council of Ministers not exceeding ten percent of Assembly members with the Chief Minister at the head aids and advises the Lieutenant Governor, though differences of opinion must be referred to the President. The Chief Minister is appointed by the President, and ministers hold office during the pleasure of the President and are collectively responsible to the Assembly.
